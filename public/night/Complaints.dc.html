How we use it

Each casino's payout speed is the median of verified reports from the last 90 days, shown once it has at least 3. A report is verified when its transaction left a wallet of that casino, and the arrival time then comes from the blockchain. Reports we cannot match are checked by hand before they count. Verified reports earn 25 points.
